Moonlit Garden Toast

from Hearth & Home · your version

20 min2 servingseasy

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough
  • 1 ripe pear, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 pinch flaky salt

Method

  1. Toast the sourdough until crisp at the edges.
  2. Layer pear and herbs over each slice.
  3. Finish with oil and flaky salt, then serve warm.

Your notes   Try it with a soft cheese next time.
